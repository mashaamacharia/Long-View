title: Mathematics modelling project - bus route costs
---

Groups modelled the weekly cost of three school bus routes using distance, fuel price and
passenger numbers. Amina's group chose to compare routes using cost per passenger rather than
total cost, a decision she proposed and defended during planning.

Rubric (out of 100): modelling choices 22/25, mathematical accuracy 21/25, written reasoning
24/25, presentation 18/25. The written reasoning section was the strongest in the class set.
